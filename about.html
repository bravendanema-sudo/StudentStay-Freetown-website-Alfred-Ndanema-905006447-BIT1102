About Us

Our Story

The project responds to the difficulty students can face when searching for safe, suitable and affordable accommodation near their place of study.

The website brings room information, facility checks and student advice together in one accessible place.

Our Mission

To make accommodation information easier for students to understand and help them ask useful questions before choosing a place.

Our Values

Safety

Students should feel safe where they live.

Community

Students can share advice and look out for each other.

Quality

Rooms should be clean, working and as described.

Support

Help is available before and after you move in.
