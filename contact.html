FAQs

How do I find a room?

Use the search on the home page or open Accommodation Listing. You can filter by location, room type and price.

Can I see a room before I pay?

Yes. Always visit the room and meet the landlord first. Ask for a receipt for every payment.

How do I request a booking?

Open a room and press Request Booking. Fill in your details and the dates you want.

How do I make an account?

Go to Student Account and choose Register.
